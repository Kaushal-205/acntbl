# Remediation and release boundaries

## Which promise can a customer buy?

PaymentGuarantee covers a single, fully funded ERC-20 transfer with an immutable recipient, payment amount, executor, premium, benefit and delivery deadline. The provider escrows the entire fixed benefit before publishing its offer. The client accepts the receipt on-chain and funds the payment and premium. The executor can deliver only to the committed recipient. Anyone can record expiry at or after the delivery deadline. Expiry credits the client with the payment, premium and fixed benefit. A beneficiary transfer that fails leaves the receipt active until successful delivery or expiry. Direct off-contract payments are outside the receipt. No delegate, hash or off-chain party can redirect the contract's delivery. A supported ERC-20 and a functioning chain are necessary to withdraw.

Example in token units: provider posts 50, customer deposits 100 plus 5. Success sends 100 to the beneficiary and credits 55 to the provider. Expiry credits 155 to the customer. The payment pool and provider reserve are distinct economic contributions. An active receipt has guaranteed capacity even at zero trading volume. This covers an observable service-level breach. Broader realized losses remain subject to the experimental epoch claims process.

## Review findings and engineering status

| Review issue | Current behavior | Remaining boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rejected, missing or timed-out report | The epoch retains capital for one correction round within a fixed outer deadline. Corrected reports get a new digest and full challenge period. A deterministic payment receipt can be settled independently. | The epoch can still void after both rounds. Arbitrator and quorum availability still matter for arbitrary workflows. |
| Unfunded promises | The pilot offers only fully reserved benefits. Experimental pools remain pro rata. | Do not market the epoch loss cap as committed capacity. |
| Unavailable evidence or incorrect claim totals | Publish evidence bytes and the complete sorted claim set on-chain before asserting. Up to 128 unique IDs per root. Contract derives the sum, root and prefix amounts; claim proofs can be rebuilt from events. | Bytes can be false or meaningless. Attesters must verify attribution, deduplication, and consistency with the actual workflow. Publish only safe evidence. A single full manifest is limited to on-chain gas capacity. |
| Incorrect or silent quorum | Pilot settlement uses immutable receipt state. Epoch challenges and replacement rounds remain available. | No universal proof of a false real-world statement. A colluding quorum and arbitrator remain trusted in the experimental model. |
| Corrections, exits and rotation | Evidence-only differences do not constitute double signing. Distinct economic decisions in one round do. Epoch snapshots isolate seats. A signer may retire, then withdraw after its last evidence window; the arbitrator may replace retired or forfeited seats. | Key loss without retirement can still degrade availability. Identity and operator independence require operational controls. |
| Premium dilution | Epoch premium funding opens after subscriptions close. The pilot requires a positive, fixed premium at acceptance. | Historical loss and willingness to pay are required for a credible quote. |
| Market incentives | An immutable gross-volume cap bounds each market. Circulating odds have no authority over the pilot. YES, NO, reserves and depth remain readable. | A volume cap cannot bound off-chain incentives or correlated positions in other markets. |
| External restaking | SymbioticRecovery supports opt-in before coverage, a time-bound capture, permissionless request and execution following a proven missed deadline, and partial, separately remitted recovery. Victim settlement runs independently. | The adapter requires authorized network middleware registration, operator opt-in, an eligible vault and a burner distributor. Actual external collections depend on available stake, veto and expiry. Fork validation on the chosen deployment remains open. |
| Multiple incidents | Each pilot execution has its own receipt, deadline and benefit. Published epoch roots bind each unique claim ID and amount. | Mixed exact and scheduled losses in one experimental report still need an agreed aggregation policy. |
| Credibility resets | Registry tracks provider-address incidents, recognized losses and seized bond across registrations. Pilot tracks deliveries, deadlines missed and benefits by provider. | A new wallet remains a new identity. Independent identity verification and risk tier calibration are separate requirements. |
| Settlement coupling | Market treasury fees accumulate as withdrawable credit. Escrow verifies receipts through the coordinator's balance reconciliation. Pilot expiry and external recovery use independent pull payments. | A paused or insolvent token can block transfers. Limit collateral to plain ERC-20s with known controls. |
| Events and dust | EpochSettled identifies capped recognized loss and evidence hash. ReportAsserted emits the typed digest. Prefix-based victim allocations pay the entire funded amount if everyone claims, independent of claim order. | Original net trading pots and vault redemptions may retain floor-rounding dust. |

## External recovery operations

Deploy one canonical adapter with the guarantee, VetoSlasher, subnetwork and distributor. Register the adapter as network middleware and configure the vault burner to send realized proceeds to the distributor. Check token, upstream epoch duration and veto window. An executor must explicitly opt in to the specific receipt before the client accepts. After expiry, request promptly and execute during the upstream slashability window. Upstream refusal or veto leaves the customer's guarantee fully funded. The distributor separately approves and remits actual collateral against the receipt, capped at the virtual amount and fixed benefit. Reconcile upstream events and transfers per slash index. An authorization, a virtual slash, and a completed token recovery have different meanings. [Upstream interface](https://github.com/symbioticfi/core/blob/main/src/interfaces/slasher/IVetoSlasher.sol)

## Economic validation

For comparable completed instructions, estimate frequency as covered failures divided by total instructions. Estimate severity in collateral units after recoveries and after applying each contracted cap. Multiply frequency by mean covered severity to estimate expected claims per instruction. Add monitored execution cost, dispute costs and the capital charge for the lock period. For an illustrative 100-unit benefit, 2% miss rate, 0.50 operating cost and 0.20 capital cost, the break-even premium is 2.70 units before tail loading. A 5-unit premium would lose money at a 10% miss rate. These are illustrative inputs. Measure per-provider concentrations and simulate a shared dependency outage, zero market volume, oracle downtime and collateral unavailability.

Collect a priced commitment from a provider, measure actual loss and recovery on independently replayable incidents, compare market forecasts with a historical baseline on held-out epochs, and obtain jurisdiction-specific advice on the selected product and customer channel. These are external validations. Contract tests alone cannot establish any of them.
